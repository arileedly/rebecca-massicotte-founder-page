About Rebecca

She didn't enter
the industry.
She rebuilt it.

Rebecca Massicotte founded Red Fox Medical Courier in 2010 after identifying a gap that the industry had no interest in closing: healthcare facilities deserved a courier partner that actually acted like one. Large national carriers were treating lab specimens like packages and lab directors like ticket numbers. Nobody picked up the phone. Nobody knew your protocols.

She built the alternative. A company where every driver is certified beyond legal requirements. Where clients have a direct line to a real operations team — not an automated menu. Where a STAT run at 3am carries the same urgency as one at noon, because the patient waiting on the other end doesn't care what time it is.

[FILL — Rebecca's own words: what specifically made you start this? A moment, a conversation, something you saw that you couldn't unsee?]

Under her leadership, Red Fox has scaled from a local Tampa operation into a multi-state network spanning Florida, Michigan, Wisconsin, New York, Georgia, Alabama, Arkansas, and beyond — growing past $5M in annual revenue while maintaining the service standards of a boutique partner.

She serves on the board of the Florida Delivery Logistics Association, has been recognized by the Children's Movement of Florida as a community business leader, and holds state and national certifications for transporting infectious samples, Class 2 pharmaceuticals, radioactive materials, and oxygen.

"We're not just moving packages — we're moving blood samples, organs, life-saving medications. Every delivery matters. Trust isn't built on big promises. It's built on showing up, every single time."
